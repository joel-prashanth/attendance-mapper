Before You Continue

Aurora Attendance requires limited access to your existing Aurora ERP session and the Google Spreadsheet you configure.

What the extension accesses

  • Your currently authenticated Aurora ERP faculty identity.
  • Your faculty timetable and attendance sessions.
  • Official ERP class rosters.
  • Attendance information from the Google Spreadsheet you explicitly configure.

Why this information is used

  • Match Google Sheet students with the official ERP roster.
  • Validate attendance before submission.
  • Preview Present and Absent counts.
  • Submit attendance only after explicit confirmation.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not store your ERP authentication cookie.
  • It does not send ERP cookies to a developer-controlled server.
  • It does not send student records or Google Sheet contents to a developer-controlled server.
  • It does not sell user data.
  • It does not use attendance information for advertising.

Attendance processing occurs inside your browser and communicates directly with Aurora ERP and the configured Google Spreadsheet.
